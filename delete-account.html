Delete Your CarryMate Account

This page explains how to request deletion of your CarryMate account and associated data.

Delete from within the app (fastest)

  1. Open the CarryMate app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is permanently deleted immediately, along with the data listed below.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email khalidbaraa@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject line “Delete my account”. We will process your request within 30 days.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove: your profile (name, email, phone number, photo), your identity-verification documents, your trips and shipments, your chat messages, and your reviews and reports.

What may be retained

We may retain a limited record where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes, for no longer than required by law. Records of transactions completed with other users may be kept in anonymized form.
